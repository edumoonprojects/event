Take it home

The Small Business Marketing Checklist

Screenshot this. Try it on a club, a family business or your first client.

Know your customer

Write one persona: name, age, what they want, how much they spend.

Post consistently

3 to 4 good posts a week beat 10 in one day and then silence.

Hook in 2 seconds

Start every reel with the most interesting thing. Add on-screen captions.

Show real people

Behind the scenes, staff and happy customers beat plain logos.

Google Business Profile

Free. Add hours, photos and the menu, and reply to every review.

Small ad tests

Target nearby, one clear offer, small budget first, then scale what works.

Reach vs results

Likes are nice. New customers through the door are the goal.

Measure weekly

Reach, clicks, messages and visits. Keep what works, drop what doesn’t.
