How to play without phones

Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Make teams of 3 or 4

Each team picks a name and writes it at the top of a sheet of paper.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Don’t mix these up

call off = cancelled“The fest is called off”
show up = arrive“Only 5 showed up”

Take it home

10 Phrasal Verbs to Use Today

Screenshot this. Use one in every conversation today.

figure out

Understand or solve. “I figured out the bug.”

put off

Delay. “Don’t put off your assignment.”

look into

Check or investigate. “I’ll look into it.”

catch up

Reach the same level. “I missed class; I’ll catch up.”

come up with

Think of an idea. “We came up with a name.”

follow up

Check again later. “I’ll follow up next week.”

run out of

Have none left. “My phone ran out of charge.”

give up

Stop trying. “Don’t give up on coding.”

turn down

Refuse, or lower volume. “She turned down the offer.”

set up

Arrange or install. “Set up a meeting.”
