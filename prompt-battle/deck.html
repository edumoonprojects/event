Take it home

8 Moves That Win Prompt Battles

Screenshot this. Try 3 moves on your next AI task.

Set the scene

Who you are, who it’s for, and why you need it.

Name the exact output

“A 6-line poster text”, “3 reel hooks”, “a 5-question quiz”.

Add constraints

Word limit, tone, language, must include, must avoid.

Show an example

One sample of the style you want beats ten adjectives.

Ask for options

“Give me 3 versions” and pick the best.

Iterate fast

“Shorter”, “funnier”, “more specific to Vizag”.

Judge like a user

Would your target person actually read, click or use it?

Check and own it

Fix facts, add your real details, make it yours.
