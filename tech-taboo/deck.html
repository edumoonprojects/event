Take it home

10 Tech Words, Simply

Screenshot this. Interviewers often ask you to explain a tech word in simple English.

Bug

A mistake in code that makes a program behave wrongly.

Loop

Code that repeats until a condition says stop.

Browser

The app you use to open websites.

Cloud

Someone else’s computers, on the internet, storing or running your stuff.

Prompt

The instruction or question you give an AI tool.

Deepfake

A fake video or voice made with AI to look real.

Phishing

A fake message that tricks you into sharing passwords or money.

Firewall

A guard that decides which network traffic is allowed in or out.

Startup

A young company trying to grow fast with a new idea.

Internship

Short-term work, often during college, to learn on the job.
