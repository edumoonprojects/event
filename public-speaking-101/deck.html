How to play without phones

Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Make teams of 3 or 4

Each team picks a name and writes it at the top of a sheet of paper.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Before Round 3 · Learn

PREP: a structure for any talk.

Point

Your main idea, in one sentence

Reason and example

Why it matters, and one real story or number

Point again

Repeat your main idea at the end, in new words

PREP

Point“Every student should do one internship.”
Reason + Example“You learn what no class teaches. My senior learned Git in a week.”
Point again“So before you graduate, do one internship.”

Round 3 · Structure · Question 3

Which line is the best way to move from your first point to your second?

A“That was the first reason. The second matters too…”
B“Umm, okay, next point, let me see what comes next…”
CJust start the next point with no link
D“Moving on, let me finish quickly.”

Clear links (transitions) tell the audience where you are. “Let me finish quickly” tells them you don’t value your own talk.
