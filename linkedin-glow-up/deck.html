How to play without phones

Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Make teams of 3 or 4

Each team picks a name and writes it at the top of a sheet of paper.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Before Round 2 · Learn

The About section: the first two lines decide if anyone reads on.

Start with a hook

What you do or build, not “I am a hardworking person”

Then your proof

2 or 3 things you’ve built, done or won

End with what you want

The kind of role or people you’d like to connect with

A simple structure

Hook“I build web apps that students actually use.”
ProofProjects, internship, hackathon
Goal“Looking for junior web developer roles.”

Before Round 5 · Learn

A good message is short, specific and easy to say yes to.

Who you are

One line: name, college, year

Why them

Something specific: their role, a post, your shared college

A small, clear ask

10 minutes of advice, not “please give me a job”

Never send

“Hi”And nothing else
“Sir please refer me”To a stranger, with no context
Your resume with no messageNobody knows why
