How to play without phones

Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Make teams of 3 or 4

Each team picks a name and writes it at the top of a sheet of paper.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Take it home

8 Small Talk Moves

Screenshot this. Use 2 at your next event.

Open with the situation

“How did you hear about this event?”

Say your name clearly

And repeat theirs: “Nice to meet you, Kiran.”

Ask open questions

What, how, why. Not yes/no.

Follow their answer

“Oh, robotics? How did you get into that?”

Share a little too

A conversation is two-way.

Listen more than you talk

Nod, react, don’t check your phone.

Avoid heavy topics at first

Salary, marks, religion, politics, looks.

Exit kindly

“Great talking to you. Let’s connect on LinkedIn!”
