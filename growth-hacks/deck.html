Waitlists build interest before launch.

Collect interest early

A simple page or form: “Join the waitlist”

Move up by inviting

Share your link to get early access sooner

Be honest

Only say “limited spots” if spots are really limited

Honest vs fake

“First 200 get early access, we can only support 200”True limit
“Only 3 spots left!” foreverFake urgency
Show the waitlist countSocial proof
