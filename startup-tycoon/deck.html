Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Before Month 3 · Learn

Know your numbers, or you’ll grow your losses.

Cost per order

Delivery person, packaging, travel

Price

What the customer pays you for each order

Profit per order

Price minus cost. If it’s negative, more orders = more loss

CampusEats’ numbers (example)

Cost per delivery₹15
Delivery fee₹20
Profit per order₹5

D₹20, it covers cost with a small profit

Below cost, every order loses money. Way above, nobody orders. Cover your cost, plus a little.

Take it home

Your First-Startup Checklist

Screenshot this. Use it the day you start your own.

Start with a real problem

One you’ve seen people complain about, not just a cool idea.

Talk to 20 people first

Before you build anything. Listen more than you pitch.

Build the smallest test

A WhatsApp group or a Google Form can be your first product.

Count repeat customers

People coming back matters more than first-time downloads.

Know your numbers

Cost per order, price, profit per order. Every week.

Spend on what you can measure

Referrals and offers you can track beat big posters.

Put the co-founder deal in writing

Who owns what, and what happens if someone leaves.

Don’t give away too much too early

Try incubators, grants and competitions before big equity deals.
