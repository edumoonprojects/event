Which Exam Leads Where

Screenshot this. Always check the official site for the current year.

GATE

M.Tech or ME in India, some PSU jobs. Engineering subject knowledge. Score valid 3 years.

CAT

MBA at IIMs and many B-schools. Verbal, reasoning and maths. Usually in November.

GRE

MS and some other programmes abroad. Verbal, maths and writing. Many universities now make it optional.

IELTS / TOEFL

English test for studying abroad. Scores valid 2 years.

GMAT

MBA abroad and some Indian schools.

UPSC CSE

IAS, IPS and more. Prelims, mains and interview. Long preparation.

Bank, SSC, state PSC

Government jobs with their own exams, like IBPS, SSC CGL and APPSC.

Always check

Dates, patterns and rules change. Use only the official website for the current year.
