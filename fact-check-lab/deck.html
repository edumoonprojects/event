Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Take it home

The 8-Step Fact Check

Screenshot this. Use it before every forward.

1. Pause

Strong feelings (shock, anger, fear) are a reason to slow down.

2. Source

Who first said it? A named, known source, or “forwarded many times”?

3. Date

Is it new, or an old story shared again?

4. Search

Search the main claim. Do trusted news sites or official pages say it too?

5. Photo

Use a reverse image search. Old photos are often reused with new stories.

6. Numbers

Do the numbers make sense? Check percentages and units.

7. Links

Check the exact website address. Look-alike names are a trick.

8. Then decide

Not sure? Don’t forward. Silence is better than spreading a fake.
