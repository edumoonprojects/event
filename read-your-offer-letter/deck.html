Before Round 1 · Learn

First check: what job, and where?

Designation

Your job title, like Associate Software Engineer

Location

Your base city, and whether you can be moved later

Offer vs letter of intent

A letter of intent is an early promise; the offer letter has the full terms

Ask HR if unclear

“Which team will I join?”
“Can the location change after joining?”
“When will I get the full offer letter?”

Service agreements (bonds): read every word.

What it is

You agree to stay a set time, or pay an amount if you leave early

Common for freshers

Especially where the company trains you

Check

How long, how much, what counts as leaving, and is it pro-rated?

Red flags

Keeping your original certificatesAsk why, and for how long
Very high amountsCompared to your salary
Vague words“As decided by management”

Take it home

Offer Letter Checklist

Screenshot this. Use it the day your first offer arrives.

Role and location

Your job title, team and city. Is relocation possible later?

Joining

Date, documents, and what happens if joining is delayed.

Probation

How long, how you are reviewed, and the notice period during it.

Notice period

How many days you must work after resigning. 30 to 90 days is common.

Bond or service agreement

How long, how much, and what exactly it covers. Read before signing.

Pay details

Fixed vs variable pay. Ask HR for the monthly in-hand estimate.

Offer validity

The last date to accept. Reply in writing.

Fake offer signs

Asking for money, a free email address, no interview, pressure to pay fast.
