Take it home

Negotiation Playbook

Screenshot this. Read it before your next deal, big or small.

Prepare

Know your target, your walk-away point and your best option if there is no deal.

Walk-away point

The worst deal you will accept. Decide it before the talk, not during.

Anchor

The first number sets the range. Make a bold but fair first offer with a reason.

Ask why

Find their interest behind their position. Cash early? Long-term order? Credit?

Trade, don’t give

“If you…, then I…”. Every discount gets something back.

Silence works

After an offer, stop talking and let them answer.

Write it down

Price, quantity, dates and payment terms in one message or document.

Win-win lasts

A deal that hurts the other side will not last. You will meet again.
