How to play without phones

Teams, paper and a pen. That’s all.

Make teams of 3 or 4

Each team picks a name and writes it at the top of a sheet of paper.

Write your answer

Write the letter (A, B, C or D). For “tap the line” questions, write the line number and the letter.

Points

100 for each correct answer. Line questions: 100 for the line + 100 for the reason. The last round counts double.

Speak up

Speaking moments earn bonus points. The host decides who gets them.

Case 2: Deepfakes. Faking a real person can cause real harm.

What it is

AI-made photos, videos or voices of real people doing things they never did

Why it’s serious

It can embarrass, harass or scam someone, and it can be illegal

If it happens to you

Don’t panic. Save proof, report on the platform and at cybercrime.gov.in or 1930

Never

Fake photos of classmatesEven “as a joke”
Fake voice of a personUsed for pranks or scams
Forward a deepfakeReport it instead

Case 4: Bias. AI can be unfair without anyone meaning it.

AI learns from past data

If the past was unfair, AI can repeat it

Examples

Favouring some names, accents, genders, colleges or languages

The fix

Test with different people, check results, keep humans responsible

Bias check

Would it treat everyone the same?Try different names
Look at the resultsWho gets left out?
Who is responsible?A human, always
