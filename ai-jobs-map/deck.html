Data analyst

Turns data into answers with Excel, SQL and dashboards. A great first AI-adjacent job.

Data engineer

Builds the pipelines that collect, clean and store data.

Uses statistics and models to find patterns and predict.

ML engineer

Trains, tests and deploys models into real products.

AI app developer

Builds apps on top of AI models and APIs: chatbots, assistants, tools.
