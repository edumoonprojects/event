8 Vizag Facts

Screenshot this. Share one fact the next time a visitor comes to Vizag.

City of Destiny

Vizag’s nickname. It is also called the Jewel of the East Coast.

INS Kursura

A real Navy submarine (1969 to 2001), now a museum on RK Beach since 2002.

Andhra University

Founded in 1926. It turns 100 in 2026.

Three hills, three faiths

A church on Ross Hill, a temple on Sringamani hillock, a dargah on Dargah Konda.

Borra Caves

Explored in 1807. “Borra” means hole in Telugu. Shaped by the Gosthani river.

Visakhapatnam Port

Opened in 1933. One of India’s 12 major ports, sheltered by Dolphin’s Nose.

Vizag Steel Plant

India’s first shore-based integrated steel plant, run by RINL.

Eastern Naval Command

The Indian Navy’s eastern headquarters is in Vizag.
